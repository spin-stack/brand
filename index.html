Snapshots and rollback

A snapshot is a point in a workspace's life that you can come back to. It lives in the installation's bucket, so it does not depend on any runner. Restore puts the disk back; --with-memory keeps the processes.

spin snapshot create demo --description clean --with-memory --wait
spin snapshot diff demo <after-id> --from <clean-id> --under /etc
A  /etc/greeting
M  /etc/hosts
D  /etc/issue
Caution. A memory snapshot resumes only on a runner that carries the same machine.

What the brand is: the mark and how it is used, and the palette, each color by what it is for. These are what palette.toml and the mark's cut hold.

The mark, then Spinbox in IBM Plex Mono 600 - the word the prose uses, capitalised, its cap height the mark's middle disk.

Not

Buttons

One primary action per view, in the accent and in words alone. A row's actions are quiet: its likely one as a secondary button and the rest behind ⋯. An icon goes alone, with a tooltip, where a toolbar has no room for words - never an icon and a word on every row. What destroys is in the destructive fill, in a confirmation, after Cancel. Every state here is a token (--spin-accent-fill-hover, --spin-control, …) that task palette derives and holds: a white label at 4.5:1 on a fill under the pointer and pressed, a hover told from rest.

Sizes

A row and a toolbar take small; a page's header medium; a dialog's footer and the site large. 44 on a touch screen.
States

A state is a badge: its shape, its word in the mono, on its own tint (--spin-success-badge and the rest, tint.badge over whatever is under it). The word holds 4.5:1 on its tint over the page, a surface and a raised fill; the shape tells it apart where the hue does not. The same badge in a table, a header and a toast.

Compositions

The same type and colours, three ways of laying a page out - each a page of this laboratory above, not a mock from the colours. Where the tokens stop and a decision begins: a token says how a border is drawn, these say where one is.

Marketing

The site's landing: a hero that says what to do before what it is, the story as one terminal, three capabilities as cards with their proof beside them and the rest as a list. The reading measure is --spin-measure; a section is a heading and what is under it, never a box.

Documentation

A Starlight page: one column at the reading measure, the sidebar at --spin-sidebar, headings in the mono. A claim links to the row of the status page that proves it.

Application

The dashboard, read for diagnosis. A page grows only as wide as what it shows: a document or a form to --spin-width-contained, one thing and what is around it to --spin-width-detail, a table of many to --spin-width-wide, an explorer to the viewport. A page is a header - the title, its state, its context and its one primary action on one row - and sections of rows between hairlines; the name beside the mark is --spin-wordmark, which the catalog audit holds the sidebar to.

Actions

One primary action per view, solid in the accent and in words alone: New workspace on a list, Open terminal on a workspace. A row's actions are quiet: its most likely one as a secondary button (the raised fill, a hairline edge, no icon) and the rest behind ⋯. An icon goes alone, with a tooltip, only where the toolbar has no room for words; never an icon and a word on every row. What destroys is in the destructive fill, in a confirmation, after Cancel.

Elevation

A menu, a dialog or a toast is on overlay with a hairline edge and --spin-shadow-overlay: a soft shadow in light; in dark only a crisp edge, since a black shadow on a near-black scrim drew a second rectangle around the dialog. The scrim covers the whole viewport.
